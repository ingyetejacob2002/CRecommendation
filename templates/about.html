A second opinion on what to plant, built from climate and soil data.

CropWise is a research prototype. It uses a machine-learning model to score ten crops against the conditions at a site in North-Central Nigeria, then ranks them.

How it works

  1. 1You supply climate and soil variables for a site.
  2. 2The model estimates suitability for each supported crop.
  3. 3Probabilities are sorted into a ranked list.
  4. 4You see the best match and the top five alternatives.
