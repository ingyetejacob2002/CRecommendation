7. INTERPRETATION
The diagnostic analysis should be interpreted before modifying the training labels or deploying the recommendation system.

A high predictive score alone does not establish that the recommendation engine is environmentally generalizable. In particular, strong crop identity importance and highly uneven crop-level suitability rates may indicate that the target variable reflects the crop requirement rules used during target construction.

The environmental-only model is included as a diagnostic baseline. If its performance is much lower than the full model, this should be examined carefully rather than automatically interpreted as evidence that environmental variables are irrelevant.

8. RECOMMENDATION
Do not overwrite the existing training dataset at this stage. Review the target-generation methodology and crop requirement thresholds before creating a corrected dataset.
